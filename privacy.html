Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

YouLetter is a personal, non-commercial tool that runs entirely on the computer of the person who uses it. This policy explains what it accesses and what happens to that information.

Information YouLetter accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant access, YouLetter uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • read the list of channels your YouTube account subscribes to;
  • read public information about recently uploaded videos on those channels (title, channel name, publish time, length);
  • create a private playlist in your account and add those videos to it.

How the information is used

The information is used only to build your daily playlist and your newsletter file. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

  • YouLetter has no server. Nothing is sent to the developer.
  • The Google access token, the list of processed videos and the newsletters are stored only in files on your own computer.
  • No information is sold, rented or shared with third parties.
  • To write summaries, the captions of the videos are processed by the AI assistant installed on your own computer under your own account with that provider.

Removing access and deleting data

  • You can revoke YouLetter's access at any time on the Google security settings page.
  • You can delete all stored data by deleting the YouLetter folder on your computer.
  • Playlists created by YouLetter stay in your YouTube account until you delete them there.

YouTube API Services

YouLetter uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your use of YouTube data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
