YouLetter

A personal daily digest of the YouTube channels you subscribe to.

What it does

  • Checks your YouTube subscriptions for videos uploaded since the last run.
  • Collects them into a private playlist for that day in your own YouTube account.
  • Writes a newsletter with a short summary and a timestamped outline for each video, saved as a file on your own computer.

How it works

YouLetter is a small script that runs on the owner's own computer. It is a personal, non-commercial tool and is not offered as a service to the public. It signs in with Google only to read the subscription list and to create the daily playlist.

Your data

YouLetter has no server and does not collect, sell or share any data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
